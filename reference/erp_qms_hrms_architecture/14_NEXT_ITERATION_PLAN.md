## Recommended Iteration Structure

### Iteration 1: Approved or near-approved governance layer

Target:

- Change Management
- Management Review
- Training and Competence
- Internal Audit

Why first:

- these govern the whole system
- they connect directly to the already-approved document and record controls

Expected outcome:

- qms and HRMS extension boundaries become much firmer
- authority presentation becomes stronger

### Iteration 2: Transaction and exception layer

Target:

- Product Realization
- Identification and Traceability
- Non-conforming Product and Rework
- CAPA
- Complaint Handling

Why next:

- these domains determine whether ERPNext and the bridge layer are sufficient
- they close the biggest remaining gap between policy and daily operations

Expected outcome:

- implementation-ready ERP transaction gates
- cleaner design for complaint, NCR, CAPA, and release evidence

### Iteration 3: Regulatory and lifecycle expansion

Target:

- PMS
- Vigilance
- Regulatory compliance
- Risk management
- Design and development if in scope

Why later:

- these are critical, but they build on the previous governance and transaction architecture

Expected outcome:

- a more complete authority-facing program pack

## Exit Criteria To Say The Research Is Fully Closed

You can say the research is fully closed only when all of the following are true:

1. all major in-scope SOP domains have been reviewed from the actual approved corpus
2. each domain has been mapped to ERPNext, `qms`, HRMS, or a clearly defined new module
3. unresolved gaps are implementation gaps, not discovery gaps
4. the authority presentation no longer depends on draft or review-stage procedures for major claims
5. the system evidence model is clear for training, traceability, complaints, NCR, CAPA, audit, management review, and release
