# Event And Integration Matrix

This matrix defines the end-state flow contract. It is intentionally concrete enough to implement.

| Flow | Triggering event | User roles | ERP document(s) | HRMS document(s) | QMS document(s) | Required evidence | Release / approval gates | Exception path | Reports / dashboards |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Document issuance, revision, obsolescence | Need for new SOP, WI, form, or revision | author, RAE, MR, approver, affected users | None required as primary transaction | employee and department data only for target audience resolution | `QMS Document`, `Obsolete Document`, `Distribution List`, `Distribution Log`, `QMS Document Activity`, `Change Request Ticket`, `Change Management` | approved file, revision, effective date, approvers, distribution, superseded document, linked impact review | review, approval, release, prior version archive, applicability resolution | change request, overdue review, obsolete-document disposal | master list, tracking list, obsolete list, acknowledgment backlog |
| Employee onboarding into controlled roles | New hire enters a controlled department, designation, or process | HR, line manager, quality admin, trainer | ERP user and employee access only after onboarding completion where needed | `Employee Onboarding`, `Employee Onboarding Template`, `Employee`, `Department`, `Designation` | new controlled-role assignment and training assignment records; current effective SOP list | onboarding tasks complete, employee active, controlled role identified, mandatory docs assigned, trainer or supervisor assigned | user cannot be enabled for sensitive roles until mandatory onboarding tasks and initial training assignment are in place | missing controlled-role setup opens quality-admin task | controlled-role onboarding queue, pending initial qualification |
| Training assignment and completion | New hire, role change, periodic recertification, or instructor-led training need | employee, line manager, quality admin, trainer | Sensitive ERP forms read training state through API | `Training Event`, `Training Result`, `Training Feedback`, `Employee`, `Department`, `Designation` | `Training Assignment`, `Training Acknowledgment`, `Competency Requirement`, `Competency Assessment`, `Authority Grant`, `Qualification Gate Log` | assignment, due date, event attendance, result, acknowledgment status, assessor, validity period, authority state | employee cannot perform listed sensitive actions until required training, acknowledgment, and competence are valid | overdue training escalates to supervisor and quality | training overdue, competency matrix, authority status, recertification due |
| SOP acknowledgment after revision | QMS document revision released and marked applicable to role or process | affected employee, line manager, quality admin | Sensitive forms display current SOP revision and acknowledgment status | active employee list from HRMS and supervisor mapping | `QMS Document`, `Training Assignment`, `Training Acknowledgment`, `Distribution Log` | document revision, effective date, assigned users, acknowledgment timestamp, overdue escalation | blocked actions for mandatory read-and-understand if acknowledgment window expired | overdue acknowledgment escalates to supervisor; repeat overdue can suspend authority | acknowledgment backlog, revision adoption, blocked-user report |
| Competency approval for sensitive operations | New operation assignment, practical assessment due, or recertification due | operator, trainer, supervisor, QA authority | `Job Card`, `Quality Inspection`, `Stock Entry`, `Delivery Note`, other sensitive ERP actions | `Employee`, `Shift Assignment`, optional `Training Result`, optional `Skill Assessment` | `Competency Requirement`, `Competency Assessment`, authority matrix, gate log | assessor, assessment result, validity dates, operation or process scope | user cannot complete sensitive operation or release until competency is current | failed assessment opens retraining or supervisor action | qualification status board, expired competency list |
| Authority mapping for release and approval roles | New approver needed, org change, delegation, or authority review | HR, quality admin, QA head, MR | release forms read authority result at time of submit | `Employee`, `Department`, `Designation`, `reports_to`, approver fields, `Department Approver` | quality authority matrix or qms org overlay | named authority holder, delegate, department or process scope, effective period | only mapped active authority can review, approve, release, concede, or close | missing authority mapping blocks release and routes to quality admin | authority coverage dashboard, delegated-authority review |
| Supplier qualification and purchasing | New supplier, requalification due, supplier quotation, PO release | buyer, supplier quality, approver | `Supplier`, `Supplier Scorecard`, `Supplier Quotation`, `Purchase Order` | no HRMS primary supplier record; HRMS only supplies approver identity where needed | new `Approved Supplier` register, linked qms supplier documents, change records | qualification evidence, scorecard state, approved item or item-group scope, due date, supplier-change review | guided or warning check on `Supplier Quotation`; hard-stop PO release only for approved supplier and item scope | supplier deviation, temporary concession, complaint, NCR | approved supplier list, requalification due, sourcing block report, blocked PO report |
| Incoming inspection and receipt release | Receipt of purchased or subcontracted product | store user, incoming QC, buyer | `Purchase Receipt`, `Subcontracting Receipt`, `Quality Inspection` | employee qualification state for inspector and release user | approved-supplier link, training state, NCR linkage | item, supplier, lot or serial, sample plan, QI result, rejected qty, inspector qualification, release authority | ERP submit blocked by invalid approved-supplier scope and by missing, draft, or rejected QI; inspector must be current where required | rejected QI auto-offers supplier deviation or NCR; supplier issue feeds scorecard and complaint trend | receipt blockers, incoming rejection trend, supplier defect dashboard, blocked receipt report |
| Manufacturing in-process control | Completion of hold-point operation | operator, production supervisor, in-process QC | `Work Order`, `Job Card`, `Quality Inspection`, `Stock Entry` | `Employee`, `Shift Assignment`, optional `Employee Checkin` | linked training, competency, NCR, CAPA, change records | operator identity, operation, readings, accepted in-process result, hold-point status, current qualification, rework evidence | server-side hold point blocks `Job Card` completion and finished-goods posting until accepted in-process inspection exists | failed check opens corrective job card and NCR | hold-point blocker board, rework trend, operation defect Pareto |
| Finished goods release and shipment release | Manufacture completion or customer shipment | final QC, warehouse, QA release authority, dispatch | `Stock Entry`, `Delivery Note`, `Sales Invoice`, `Batch`, `Serial No`, `Quality Inspection`, `Work Order`, `Job Card` | active authority holder and current qualification state | new `Release Dossier` / DHR packet, linked controlled spec and release checklist, linked unresolved exception state | final QI, lot or serial identity, source execution chain, unresolved deviation or complaint status, release approver, authority check result | batch or serial release authorization before unrestricted shipment; dossier cannot freeze unless evidence set and exception state are justified | failed release creates NCR or concession workflow | pending release dossier queue, shipment hold report, traceability retrieval |
| Complaint intake and escalation | Customer issue, service report, field failure, return | support, service, quality, RAE | `Issue` as primary complaint register; linked `Warranty Claim`, `Maintenance Visit`, `Installation Note`, return docs, and delivery evidence | escalation owner, supervisor, department owner, service employee context | complaint-classification extension on `Issue`, linked `Non Conformance`, linked `Quality Action`, linked external evidence where needed | customer context, item, serial or batch, delivery link, severity, reportability screen, investigation, service outcome, owner assignment | complaint cannot close without classification, product identification, investigation summary, and either justified no-escalation or linked NCR/CAPA | recurrent or serious complaint opens NCR and CAPA; owner and supervisor resolved from HRMS | complaint aging, severity trend, complaint-to-NCR conversion, field-failure dashboard |
| NCR / CAPA lifecycle | Rejected QI, complaint, audit finding, supplier issue, release deviation | quality engineer, process owner, approver, supervisor | source ERP transaction plus extended `Non Conformance`, `Quality Action`, and `Quality Action Resolution` | owner, approver, department, supervisor, and role history from HRMS | linked training impact, change impact, effectiveness review, management review references | containment, disposition, root cause, action plan, effectiveness evidence, closure approval, responsible person | closure blocked until containment, disposition, action completion, effectiveness, and responsible authority are complete and valid | repeat issue escalates to management review and supplier or change action | NCR aging, CAPA effectiveness due, recurrence trend, overdue closure board |
| Role change, department transfer, and exit | Transfer, designation change, department change, relieving date, or separation | HR, line manager, quality admin, IT, QA | ERP permissions and sensitive-role access reviewed | `Employee Transfer`, `Employee Separation`, `Employee`, `Department`, `Designation` | reassignment, revocation, retraining, authority-review tasks | old authority revoked, new role evaluated, mandatory retraining assigned, open quality ownership reassigned | sensitive actions blocked until new qualification is valid; separation blocks future approvals | access-review backlog, reassignment exceptions, pending authority cleanup |
| Change control affecting docs, process, or product | Proposed document or process change, deviation, CAPA output | requester, reviewer, approver, impacted owners | linked ERP records when change impacts item, BOM, operation, inspection template, or routing | impacted controlled roles and training audiences from HRMS | `Change Request Ticket`, `Change Management`, `Questionnaire`, `QMS Document`, training impact records | reason, impact answers, affected docs, implementation evidence, new version links, retraining impact | no implementation close until evidence, linked revisions, and training impact are complete | blocked if impacted document is not released or retraining unresolved | change pipeline, overdue implementation, open impact actions |
| Management review and audit evidence aggregation | Scheduled review cadence or audit preparation | management representative, QA head, function owners | `Quality Goal`, `Quality Review`, `Quality Meeting`, ERP operational reports | HRMS training, lifecycle, and authority reports | review pack in `qms`, audit module, document and training dashboards | KPI set, complaints, supplier quality, training status, authority gaps, open CAPA, change status, release issues | meeting cannot close without required input pack and action capture | overdue action remains visible in next review cycle | management review pack, audit pack, action closure dashboard |
